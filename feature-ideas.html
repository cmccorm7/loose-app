Loose — Product Roadmap

Where we track what's coming next for the Loose hexagon animation app. Ideas move from exploration through development as they take shape.

Exploring 4 ideas
Creative
Custom Color Palettes
Let users build their own hexagon color schemes beyond the default yellow, purple, and black. Includes a live preview swatch that animates as you pick.
12 votes
UX
Gesture Controls
Swipe to steer the animation direction. Pinch to resize hexagons on the fly. Long-press to freeze a single hexagon while others keep moving.
9 votes
Fun
Sound Design
Audio feedback during each animation phase — a rising tone while hexagons gather, a rattle during the shake, and a satisfying scatter sound on the fall.
7 votes
Tech
Physics Playground
Expose gravity, bounce, and friction as sliders. Watch how different physics parameters change the way hexagons fall and scatter.
6 votes
Next Up 3 ideas

Share as GIF
Record a loop of the current animation and export it as a shareable GIF or short video. One tap to capture, one tap to send.

Formation Patterns

Home Screen Widget
A live Android widget that plays a miniature hexagon animation right on the home screen. Tap to launch the full app.

Dream Big 3 ideas

AR Hexagons
Use the device camera to project hexagon animations onto real surfaces. Watch them gather on your desk, shake, and scatter across the floor.
22 votes
Social
Multiplayer Sync
Connect two devices over the network so their hexagon animations play in sync. One person's hexagons scatter onto the other person's screen.
19 votes

Downloadable visual themes that change colors, shapes, and animation behavior all at once. Neon, ocean, retro arcade, and more — each with its own personality.

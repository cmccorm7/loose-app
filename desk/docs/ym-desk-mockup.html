Position size

You choose what the trade may cost. The stop decides how many contracts that buys.

A twenty-point stop on MYM is ten dollars, and smaller than a typical five-minute bar. That is fine directly under a level that has held, where being stopped means the level broke. In open price it is a coin flip on noise.

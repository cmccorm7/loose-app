How it works: On Robinhood, you buy event contracts priced at the market's implied probability. Buy at $0.85 → profit $0.15 if the team wins, lose $0.85 if they don't. This tool compares our model's win probability against the market price to find edge, then runs 10,000 simulated seasons to project your P&L.
